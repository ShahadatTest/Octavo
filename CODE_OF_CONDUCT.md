# Code of conduct

Octavo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, and criticise ideas and code, not people.
Everyone here is giving their spare time.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and sharing pirated books or links to them, in issues or anywhere
else in the project.

## Reporting

On the comment, issue or pull request, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Report content**, then report
it to the repository maintainers. The report reaches the maintainer privately. You can also report
anything to GitHub directly from the same menu.

Reports get a reply within a week. Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private
word to removing content, locking a thread or blocking someone from the project, following the
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
